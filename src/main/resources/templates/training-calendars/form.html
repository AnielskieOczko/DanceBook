How to connect your Google Calendar:

  1. Open Google Calendar settings for the calendar you want to connect.
  2. Under Share with specific people or groups, share it with DanceBook's service account.
  3. Grant "Make changes to events" so DanceBook can save new sessions to this calendar.
  4. Copy the Calendar ID from the Integrate calendar section and paste it below.
