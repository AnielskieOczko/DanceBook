Issue #427. Today the first screen holds title, Add Session, a five-tab bar that overflows, the calendar selector with its cog, a Sync button, and search with Filters: seven stacked rows before the first session. Three ways to cut that down, each shown at 375 px with the List view and its first rows.

Kept in every option: title and "11 sessions logged", Add Session, the five views (List, Calendar, Timeline, Stats, History), the calendar selector, calendar settings, Sync now with its "Synced 21:00" time, search and Filters. Fixed in every option: the tab bar has no scrollbar and no label is cut off; the five views always fit on one line.

A · One tidy header, fewer rows star Recommended

  • Title row: title and count on the left, Add Session on the right as a compact pill (icon and "Add"). Same right-aligned action position as desktop and #424.
  • Views: an underlined text tab strip, five equal columns, no icons, no scrolling.
  • Calendar line: the calendar selector chip, a sync icon button and the settings cog share one line; "Synced 21:00" moves under the chip as a caption.
  • Search + Filters stay as in the mobile-lists mock.
  • Cost: four rows instead of seven. Nothing is hidden. No new component beyond a compact button.
