Bronze, Silver and Gold

Each direction sits next to the letter class (H to S) and never replaces it. A figure or choreography with no medal shows nothing. All five use muted metals, so the teal accent stays the only strong colour on the page.

Recommendation: direction A, Rank bars

Three ascending bars with one, two or three filled. The count carries the level and it still reads in greyscale at 12px. Spell the word out (Bronze, Silver, Gold) wherever there is room: headers and choreography titles. In dense rows the bars stand alone, with the word in the tooltip.

D, Medal icon, is the friendliest and the only one that looks like a medal. Its numeral is podium order: Gold 1, Silver 2, Bronze 3. It loses to A in one place. At 16px the numeral is about 7px tall and blurs, so you read the level from a digit and do not see it at a glance. At 12px, with the ribbon dropped, coin and numeral are readable but still slower than counting bars. Pairing D in headers (24px and up, with the word) and A in dense rows is a sound option.

D2, the same medal with no numeral, is the cleanest to look at, but on the coin colour is the only cue. In greyscale the three metals collapse into near-identical greys and the three coins become one coin. It is not safe on its own. It needs a redundant cue: always show the word (Bronze, Silver, Gold) beside it, or add a ribbon notch count. In dense rows, where there is no room for the word, I would not use D2.

B has a strong medal feel, but circle and diamond are close at 12px. C is clear where there is room, but the word makes every row wider.

Decisions needed

  1. Pick a medal direction, or a pairing such as A in rows and D in headers.
  2. Resolved: the numeral on D is podium order (Gold 1, Silver 2, Bronze 3). One thing to confirm: the medal picker lists Bronze, Silver, Gold as rising levels, so with D the digits on its options run 3, 2, 1 left to right. The word is always beside the glyph in the picker, so I left the order as levels, but it is a visible mismatch. Say if you would rather the picker list Gold first.
  3. If D2 is used, accept that it needs the word beside it, or choose a ribbon notch count as the second cue.
  4. Confirm Side by side as the third step view next to Leader and Follower.
  5. Confirm that dense step inputs are 36px tall on desktop (44px on a phone), below the 48px of the rounded-forms standard, because eight fields per row do not fit at 48px.
  6. Is the medal set by hand by the figure's editor, or derived from a syllabus level? The mock assumes a hand-set value with None as the default.
  7. Should a choreography carry its own medal, or show the highest medal among its entries? The mock shows its own value.
