Training timeline: three directions

Today the Training page's timeline reads like the calendar turned on its side: one card per session, month after month. These three directions each answer a different question. Where am I in the season? What did the week add up to? How consistent have I been, and what happened on that one day? Each is interactive and shown at desktop width and in a 375px phone frame. Every session carries the full detail the app records. The three directions share one dataset, so you can compare them directly.

Status: direction 2 (journey feed) is the provisional pick while direction 4 is explored. Direction 4 combines the cluster bubbles, icons and zoom of 1 with the weekly rollup and streak markers of 2, drawn as graphics, with no horizontal scrolling. Mock data runs 4 May to 1 Nov 2026, with today set to Sun 11 Oct. It includes weekly series, a workshop, a competition, a three-day camp, skipped and cancelled sessions, and past sessions still unconfirmed. Dashed medal slots mark where badges from #35 and #39 will go. The badge designs themselves belong to those issues. Try the "I attended" and "I skipped" buttons on an unconfirmed session and watch all views update.

1 · Zoomable horizontal timeline with session clusters

One time axis for the whole season. Zoom between Season, Month and Week and the sessions regroup: a bubble per week, then per day, then individual cards. Click a cluster to read its sessions underneath.

Desktop
375px

What it is good at

  • Shows rhythm and gaps at a glance: holidays, camp week, and the competition spike are visible without reading anything.
  • Cluster size encodes hours, fill encodes outcome, so attendance reads before any label does.
  • The top lane carries streak milestones, which is where #39 badges would pin to a date.
  • Trade-off: on a phone the track is a horizontal scroll, and exact details are always one tap away in the panel below.

2 · Vertical journey feed with weekly rollups and streak markers

The closest to today's timeline, but each week gets a summary and streak milestones interrupt the list. Newest first, with a Today line. Tap a session to expand its full detail.

Desktop
375px

What it is good at

  • Tells a story: "this week you trained 3.5 h and kept a streak of 10".
  • The weekly rollup pins beside the sessions on desktop and sits on top of them on a phone, so it never costs extra width.
  • Streak markers are natural homes for badge awards (#35, #39): the dashed slot turns into the earned badge.
  • Trade-off: it is the longest page of the three, so older history still needs "load older".

3 · Season heat-strip with drill-down into a day

The whole season is one grid of days, shaded by minutes trained. Click a day for its sessions. On a phone the grid turns vertical (weeks as rows) and the drill-down becomes a bottom sheet.

Desktop
375px

What it is good at

  • The densest view: a whole season fits on one screen and consistency is visible as texture.
  • Outcomes keep distinct marks, so colour never carries the meaning alone: skipped is an outlined cell, cancelled is struck through, unconfirmed has a warning pip, upcoming is hatched.
  • Drill-down reuses the same session detail as the session page, with Note link, segments and attendance actions.
  • Trade-off: titles are hidden until you open a day.

4 · Fitted bubbles: direction 1 meets direction 2 exploring

Same bubbles, icons, streak lane and Season / Month / Week zoom as direction 1, but the range always fits the width: weeks across on desktop, a vertical spine on a phone. Weekly rollups and streaks are small graphics (rings, bars, icon plus number). Use the arrows to move through months and weeks; tap anything for the full session.

Desktop
375px

What it is good at

  • No sideways scrolling at any width: zoom changes the range, arrows move the window.
  • Icon = session kind (style, competition, camp, workshop); size = time trained; corner mark = outcome; coloured dots = styles.
  • Ring = attendance for the week, bar = hours; the dashed medals in the streak lane are the #35 / #39 slots.
  • Trade-off: the exact times and titles live in the tap panel, not on the chart.
